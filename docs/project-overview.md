# Zeara — Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

Zeara is an all-in-one fitness tracking platform designed to help users plan their workout routines, record training sessions, and analyze their fitness progress.

The platform aims to bring essential workout management tools into one organized and accessible application.

## 2. Background

People who regularly engage in fitness activities often need to manage several aspects of their training, including workout schedules, exercise records, and performance progress.

Keeping these records organized can become difficult when using separate applications or manual tracking methods.

Zeara aims to address this by providing an integrated platform for workout planning, tracking, and progress analysis.

## 3. Project Vision

To provide a simple and organized platform that helps people manage their workout activities and understand their training progress.

## 4. Target Users

Zeara is designed for:

- Gym enthusiasts who want to record their workouts.
- Beginners who need help organizing their training routines.
- Regular exercisers who want to monitor their performance.
- People who want to maintain structured workout records.

## 5. Core Product Pillars

### PLAN

Help users organize their training through pre-built workout templates and customizable routines.

### TRACK

Allow users to record workout sessions, exercises, sets, repetitions, and weights.

### ANALYZE

Help users understand their training progress through statistics, personal records, training volume, and workout frequency.

## 6. Product Scope

The initial version of Zeara will focus on:

- User authentication and account management.
- Workout routine management.
- Built-in and custom exercise libraries.
- Workout session logging.
- Workout history.
- Basic progress analytics.
- User profiles and settings.

## 7. Design Philosophy

Zeara follows a Y2K Webcore / Retro Internet aesthetic inspired by early 2000s digital interfaces.

The design combines nostalgic visual elements with modern usability principles.

Key visual characteristics:

- Pastel baby blue, pink, lavender, and white.
- Retro computer windows and interface elements.
- Pixel-inspired icons and decorative details.
- Glossy surfaces and chrome-inspired accents.
- Playful typography.

The interface should remain readable, intuitive, and responsive, especially when displaying workout statistics and training records.

## 8. Project Goals

1. Build a functional fitness tracking web application.
2. Provide an integrated experience for planning, tracking, and analyzing workouts.
3. Develop practical skills in full-stack web development.
4. Practice the complete software development lifecycle, from planning and design to deployment.
5. Create a project that can be extended through collaboration and future improvements.

## 9. Current Status

**Phase:** Planning and UI/UX Design

The project is currently focused on defining requirements, establishing the design direction, and preparing the initial application structure.

## 10. Project Motto
